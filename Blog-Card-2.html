Life Beneath the Blue Oceans

Published on January 31, 2026

Oceans cover more than 70% of the Earth’s surface and play a crucial role in supporting life on our planet. They regulate climate, provide food, and support millions of species.

Marine ecosystems such as coral reefs, mangroves, and deep-sea habitats are rich in biodiversity. These ecosystems help maintain balance in the natural world.

Unfortunately, pollution, plastic waste, and climate change threaten ocean life. Protecting oceans is essential to ensure a healthy planet for future generations.
